▶ Playbook — timing, targeting & SA notes
Timing & schedule
  • Run campaigns 24/7 — don't restrict hours manually; the algorithm optimises delivery better than a manual schedule.
  • SA → US timezone gap: US buyers are active while you sleep. Check metrics once each morning rather than watching them live — reacting mid-learning-phase is how good ads get killed early.
Targeting
  • Target Tier-1 English markets: US / UK / CA / AU.
  • One country per ad set — start with the US.
  • Start broad: no interest-stacking. Let the pixel find the buyer.
  • Minimum 3–5 creative variants in Tier 1 — you're testing hooks, not one ad.
Scaling discipline
  • Scale winners by +20–30% budget every 2–3 days.
  • Never double a budget — it resets the learning phase and spikes CPA.
South Africa specifics
  • Shopify Payments unavailable in SA → use PayFast or Yoco as gateway.
  • Currency edge: revenue in USD, costs largely in ZAR — every $1 of margin is worth ~R18+ locally. The roll-up shows both.
  • Backend stubs waiting on Railway: /api/product-import (CJ feed), /api/shopify-publish (Admin API per store), /api/facebook-ads (Insights auto-fill).
